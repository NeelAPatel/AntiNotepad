## Behavior changes

- Gate success now requires Selftest PASS. A successful build without `AntiNotepad.exe` is Selftest FAIL; after a failed build, Selftest remains SKIP.
- `-Offline` adds the WARN line `Offline: NuGet audit off (sandbox)` and passes `NuGetAudit=false` to the build only. Without the switch, the property and Offline step are omitted.
- Size warnings now fire when a changed C# file crosses `-SizeWarn`, or grows by more than `-SizeGrowth` while already over the limit. `-SizeGrowth` defaults to 25 lines.
- Product behavior: None.
