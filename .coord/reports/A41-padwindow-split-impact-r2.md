## 6. Value check

**The full original scope is not compelling as one task.** This remains a 2,058-line presentation owner; moving timer, clipboard and navigation orchestration mostly redistributes coupled code. A40 already solved the settings-panel dependency and exposed a small ScheduleSave seam (`W:555`; `L:493`; `N:404`; `K:285`; `B:321`; `ST:119`; `W:364-367`).

Smallest worthwhile slice: **A41-padwindow-split-hotkeys**, with fakeable swap/restore capability checks and ownership guards. The ~52 method lines at `W:441-492`, slots/status at `W:29,35-36`, and disposal at `W:281-282` form a real lifetime boundary. Forwarding glue offsets the reduction; expect tens of W lines saved, not a transformation of the total. Its value is testing rollback independently of focus/native registration, not achieving a line quota.

Clipboard policy is a reasonable second slice if preventing privacy/dedupe regressions is the immediate goal, but only a few lines are pure (`N:310-315`). Skip the scheduler wrapper unless timer ownership itself is causing problems: debounce is already isolated and lifecycle gates intentionally differ (`W:364-401`). Skip navigation, taskbar, trims, timer attention and OCR in this task for the entanglement/size reasons in section 3. The plan's W≤430 / total≈1850 targets were estimates against the old baseline and should not be acceptance criteria now (`.coord/tasks/A41-padwindow-split-plan.md:66`; current counts above).
