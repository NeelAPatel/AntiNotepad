## Visual QA and limits

- Repaired Card's clipped final text by revising vertical padding and reducing body to 16 DIP. The final main/bars/menu frames show the complete fixture including strike and last paragraph.
- Direct 144-DPI RTB rendering double-scaled nested card text and omitted menu/control children. Rejected those frames. Final renderer uses a fixed 960×800-DIP tree inside a Viewbox, explicitly scales the vector tree to 1×/1.5× and rasterizes via RTB at 96 DPI. This is new vector rasterization, not bitmap upsampling. Both scales now show the complete menu and consistent layout.
- Caret is real, shown after a render invalidation flush, at line 13 / column 21 in the indented task text. Empty states use a real empty document with a hint overlay; status reports line 1 / column 1 and zero words in Pro.
- Native Windows title bar/DWM shadow/corners and compositor backdrops cannot be captured by the client-tree RTB. Internal Card shadow/radii are actual opaque Avalonia Borders. No simulated desktop/backdrop is painted.
- Natural editor leading and flush-left code-panel text remain. Custom chevrons replace the harsh stock boxes without changing the installed folding manager. Offset-only scrolling/redraw, folding interaction and pointer target feel need hands-on validation before production integration.
- Main shows subdued bar opacity; bars shows full opacity and a More hover treatment. Hover-reveal and motion are proposals, intentionally not implemented in this design-selection task.
- Carousel frames are deterministic intermediate translations of two real editors; resting Card neighbour peeks are edge surfaces. They do not measure live animation quality. Card's dot range beyond eight notes still needs a production policy.
- The design host is a fixture/review mode, not a replacement resident shell: it never opens real notes or registers hotkeys/tray. More/pin/minimize/hide are real controls; New/Delete/navigation/menu-command layouts, rail search, and Settings are design affordances awaiting selected-direction integration. The in-tree menu is deliberate so RTB includes it; native popup behavior is not being claimed.
