# AntiNotepad

A Windows scratchpad and my project for learning how to build with AI.

- I want a translucent pad that appears quickly when I need to write and gets out of the way when I am done.
- I am also learning about AI models, agentic development, orchestration, PStack and the development loop. AI leads much of the work; I scrutinize plans, results and hand tests.
- Jfung of [Antinote](https://antinote.io) gave me permission to use it as inspiration and for branding and styling, subject to change on request.

- Explore my process in [[Dev Loop Overview]], [[Roles and a Worked Example]] and [[Building with AI and Lessons]].
- See [[Architecture and Data]] and [[Decisions and Trade-Offs]] for the design and its limits.
