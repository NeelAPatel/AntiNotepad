A20 unify lab: one smooth feel for scroll, swipe and overscroll

Five notes. Scroll up and down inside a note, swipe left and right between them, and pull past the first or last note (or the top or bottom of a note). Mouse wheel, touchpad or drag all work. "Today's app" is a port of what the app does now, so you can feel the three separate behaviors. "Unified" is one engine for all of it.
