A29 shortcuts lab

A workspace to decide which shortcuts the app offers, which to remove, and the keys, before anything is built. Click a key box and press the keys. Press Record, then press the keys. The browser keeps a few combos for itself (Ctrl+N, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+W, Alt+Left); for those, tick the Ctrl, Alt or Shift chips in the bar first, then press only the plain key (tick Ctrl, press N, get Ctrl+N). Ctrl+Alt+Shift with 1 to 9 or F1 to F9 is refused: dev copies own them. Your choices save in this browser; Export gives text to paste back to the item chat.

What is already taken (the recorder refuses these)
  • Plain typing, Space, Enter, Tab, Backspace, Delete, Insert, arrows, Home, End, PageUp, PageDown.
  • Shift + arrows, Home, End, PageUp/Down, Insert, Delete: select text, cut, paste.
  • Ctrl + arrows, Home, End, Backspace, Delete, Insert: word and document movement and deletion.
  • Ctrl + A, C, X, V, Z, Y, D, I and Ctrl+Shift+Z: select all, clipboard, undo/redo, delete line, indent.
  • Alt+Shift + arrows, Home, End and Ctrl+Alt+Shift + Left/Right: rectangular selection.
  • Alt+F4, Alt+Tab, Alt+Space, Alt+Esc, Ctrl+Esc, PrintScreen, Win keys: Windows.
  • Ctrl+Alt+Shift + 1 to 9, F1 to F9 and S: dev copies and the global show/hide.
  • Allowed but flagged: Ctrl+W, S, O, P, B, U, K, H, Tab, Shift+C, Shift+Q (other apps use them).
