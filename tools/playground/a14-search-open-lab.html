A14-search-open-fade-up: search open and close

Click Search in a pad to open it; click again (or Esc key, or Close) to close it. Left = today (opens with a 120 ms fade, closes instantly). Right = the proposal. Curves and durations are from Microsoft Learn "Timing and easing" (Fluent motion): enter = Fast Out, Slow In cubic-bezier(0,0,0,1); exit = Slow Out, Fast In cubic-bezier(1,0,1,1); durations 83 / 167 / 250 ms. Microsoft's own examples make exits shorter than entrances and slide up on the way in.
