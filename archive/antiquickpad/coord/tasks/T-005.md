## M4 carousel
- Horizontal wheel (MX Master 3S thumb wheel; also Shift+vertical wheel): ONE notch = previous/next note, ~220 ms eased slide. Coalesce a burst so one notch never skips two notes; queue at most one pending step.
- Mouse drag on a grab area (not the text body; e.g. a thin strip, or Alt+drag on the body) = 1:1 direct manipulation, release snaps to nearest note with velocity threshold.
- Small page indicator (dots or "3/7"), fades when idle. Ctrl+N new note (exists), plus delete-note command with confirmation-free undo is NOT required; just a delete shortcut that keeps at least one note.
- Switching notes preserves each note's caret/scroll; persistence (M2) unchanged.
- Selftest: notch -> index+1 exactly once; burst -> no double skip; drag past threshold snaps, short drag returns.
